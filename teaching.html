2025 REU materials

The materials below are a series of problem sets developed for the group I co-mentored with Ansuman Bardalai during the 2025 Berkeley Summer REU. The problem sets develop, in an inquiry-based style, fundamental concepts in geometric representation theory, including the spherical and affine Bruhat decompositions. They assume a first course in linear algebra, abstract algebra, and some basic notions from topology and measure theory. The final item is a problem set on nonarchimedean orbital integrals used to initiate the research portion of the REU.
