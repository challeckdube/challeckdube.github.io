Research

Research & publications

Geometric representation theory, the Langlands program, and the geometry of moduli spaces.

Research interests

My interests center on Hitchin fibrations, endoscopy, and affine Springer fibers, including their role in applications to trace formulae. I am also interested in the geometrization of nonarchimedean local representation theory, as well as the theory of singular support and characteristic cycles in positive characteristic. A slightly-out of date research statement can be found here.
