Research

Research & publications

Geometric representation theory, the Langlands program, and the geometry of moduli spaces.

Research interests

My interests center on Hitchin fibrations, endoscopy, and affine Springer fibers, including their role in the weighted fundamental lemma. I am also interested in singular support and characteristic cycles on affine Grassmannians in positive characteristic.
