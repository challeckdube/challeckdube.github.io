Mathematics

Connor Halleck-Dube

I study geometric representation theory and the Langlands program.

My research interests include Hitchin fibrations, affine Springer fibers, and the geometry underlying the fundamental lemma. I am also interested in singular support and characteristic cycles in positive characteristic.

Research

I am interested in the interaction between algebraic geometry, representation theory, and harmonic analysis, particularly through the geometry of moduli spaces.

  • Hitchin fibrations and endoscopy
  • Weighted fundamental lemmas
  • Affine Grassmannians and characteristic cycles
