Research

My research broadly centers around applications of geometric representation theory and the geometry of moduli to arithmetic. In particular, my research interests include

  • Hitchin fibrations, affine Springer fibers, and endoscopy;
  • affine Deligne--Lusztig theory and nonarchimedean local representations; and
  • microlocal theory in positive characteristic.
